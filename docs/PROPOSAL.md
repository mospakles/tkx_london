# TKX London website — itemised proposal

Prepared 22 October 2026 for the TKX London owner. All figures are in **GBP** unless stated.

The **£5,000** budget covers the whole business launch, not just the website. This proposal keeps the website to well under half of it, so photography, stock and marketing keep their share.

## How to read the figures

- **Verified:** a third-party price checked on the vendor's official site on 22 Oct 2026. VAT treatment is shown where the vendor states it.
- **Estimate:** developer time. Estimates assume a **£300/day** rate. That rate is a placeholder for the owner and developer to agree; it isn't a quote.
- **Unverified:** no vendor quote has been invented. Get a quote.

## Recommendation

| Decision | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | **Shopify, Basic plan** | The owner can manage products, stock, orders and content alone. Hosted checkout, multi-currency (Markets), launch-list capture and order emails are all built in. Basic includes Markets and per-market currencies. Higher plans mainly add multi-currency payouts and lower card rates, which a launch-size shop doesn't need. |
| Theme | **The custom lean OS 2.0 theme in this repo, already built** | The brief needs matching sets with independent sizes, Shop the Look with a combined total, a pre-launch switch and a distinctive editorial layout. On a free theme such as Dawn or Horizon, those features would need custom sections anyway. The core theme already exists and passes Theme Check, so the remaining cost is configuration, content and testing, not a rebuild. |
| Alternative if scope must shrink | A free Shopify theme plus the free **Shopify Bundles** app | Cheaper to maintain, but the look is closer to a template. With Bundles, a set is a single bag line and can't be combined with pre-orders. |
| Headless or custom checkout | **Not recommended** | No requirement justifies it. It would cost more and take ownership away from the owner. |

## F. Post-launch support and handover

- **Bug fixes:** 30 days of fixes to delivered features are included.
- **Handover session:** a 90-minute recorded session.
- **Handover package:**
  - final theme source (this repository)
  - asset and licence inventory
  - configuration notes
  - the owner guide
  - the outstanding-task list

All accounts stay in the owner's name: domain, Shopify, Payments and email. The developer uses collaborator access, which the owner can remove at any time.

## Assumptions

- UK-registered merchant, eligible for Shopify Payments.
- Up to about 15 products at launch, supplied with complete specs and photography by the deadlines above.
- English only.
- The developer doesn't write legal policies or make tax decisions.
- If deadlines slip, launch wording is updated rather than launching with placeholders.
