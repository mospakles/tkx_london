## Replacement checklist (product content)

For each launch product:

- [ ] Final name and description (approved copy)
- [ ] Price (GBP), SKU per variant, stock per colour and size
- [ ] Studio images: **front, back, detail** at least 2000 px on the long edge, the same aspect ratio (3:4 recommended), on a consistent background
- [ ] Movement video (optional): 5–10 s, muted, MP4, under 10 MB
- [ ] Model height and size worn (for each model shown)
- [ ] Fit notes
- [ ] Garment measurements per size (cm), from the graded spec
- [ ] Fabric composition, exactly as on the label
- [ ] Care instructions, from the care label
- [ ] Verified pocket, waistband and fastening details
- [ ] Pre-order dispatch window (pre-order variants only)
- [ ] Image alt text

**Do not publish** support, compression, opacity, performance or sustainability claims without written evidence from the manufacturer or testing.

## Campaign and editorial imagery

- [ ] Hero: a landscape image (desktop) **and** a portrait crop (mobile) showing full silhouettes. Or a muted loop video with a poster image.
- [ ] Shop the Look: one full-length outfit image per look
- [ ] Considered Details: four macro shots (fabric, seams/waistband, pockets, tonal branding near hem or pocket)
- [ ] Editorial: five lifestyle images (everyday plans, travel, relaxed work, movement, weekend)
- [ ] Our Story image
- [ ] Pre-launch campaign image

**Casting and styling:** casting should include different body shapes and skin tones, as the brief requires. Garment branding stays subtle and tonal.

**Licences:** for every photo or video, record the photographer, the usage rights (web, social, paid ads, duration) and model releases.
